# Flight Research

Use with `SKILL.md` for flight and travel research.
Worked example in Jorge's vault: `home/japan-trip-2026/` (`brief.md`, `progress.md`, `findings/`).

## 1. Brief

Build `brief.md` before planning searches. Ask Jorge for anything missing in one message; these answers changed results in the Japan run.

- **How we operate:** what to optimize (usually price), stops, cabin, cash/points mixing, research-only.
- **Travelers:** home airport and fixed obligations per person.
- **Hard constraints:** a date table of where each person must be; the departure and return date windows.
- **Decisions:**
  - fare class (is Basic OK?);
  - checked bags per person, including regional legs;
  - whether travelers may split, and for what saving;
  - the seat-fee limit for sitting together, short-haul and long-haul;
  - the points spending cap and the minimum value per point (¢/pt);
  - alternative origins and positioning flights;
  - destination airports and open-jaw.
- **Points and accounts:** balances, expiries, and the bank-portal redemption rate.
- **Flights to book**, per traveler and direction.
- **Booking rules:** no skipped segments or hidden-city plans; per-traveler options to price (cash, points, mixed, positioning); ±days to check; carriers beyond the obvious one.
- **Where to search, priorities, deliverable.**

Mark "typically/likely/(verify)" claims as assumptions to confirm.

## 2. Search plan

- Enumerate legs: traveler × direction × date window × airports, including positioning flights and open-jaw.
- Workers, by role:
  - **cash long-haul**;
  - **award long-haul**, including the bank portal; it reads the cash worker's file for ¢/pt;
  - **regional/domestic**, cash and points;
  - **policy/rules**, which mostly needs fetch tools, not a browser.
- List every login the plan needs (airlines, loyalty programs, bank portal) and ask Jorge for all of them in one message during preflight.

## 3. Where to search

- **Cash:** find fares on Google Flights (multi-city) and ITA Matrix. Confirm the exact fare, bag fee, and seat fee on the airline site; adjacent-seat prices only show on the seat map.
- **Awards:** find space on seats.aero, point.me, or Roame. Their data is cached, so confirm on the program's site before treating a seat as bookable.
- **Bank portal (Chase Travel):** get real quotes for the exact itineraries. Read the rate banner and how many points qualify for the higher rate, and check Points Boost.
- **Award charts:** when live search is blocked, price from the program's published chart and label the result assumed.
- **Policies:** official airline and program pages.
- **Blocked sites:** aa.com, united.com, Aeroplan, and seats.aero blocked automation browsers but worked in Jorge's Chrome. Some carrier sites block even that (HTTP 403, Access Denied, or clicks that never register). Record the blocker, use the Google Flights price labeled unverified, and move on.

## 5. Pricing traps

- One-way cash fares are often far above half a round trip. An award one-way looks strong against that, but then the other direction is an expensive cash one-way. Compare whole-trip totals.
- Compute ¢/pt against the cheapest matching cash fare, never an inflated one-way.
- Partner awards can carry carrier surcharges (BA Avios on AA long-haul added $354–396 per person per segment).
- A bank portal's fixed rate can beat every transfer partner; price it before recommending transfers.
- Fares from an airline hub can cost more than connecting fares from elsewhere; a positioning flight on another carrier may win.
- Party size can change the price; confirm with a search for the full party.
- Basic fares vary: some include a long-haul checked bag, and most charge for seats.

## 6. Stop lines

- Stop at the seat map or before payment. Enter traveler names only with Jorge's OK.
- Avoid carts. If a quote needs one, report the item so Jorge can clear it.
- Never transfer points; transfers are irreversible. Confirm the award seat is bookable, then ask Jorge.
